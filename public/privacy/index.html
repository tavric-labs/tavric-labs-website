Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy covers two things Tavric Labs operates: this website, and Cully, our automation bot. Cully publishes content that Tavric Labs has written to Tavric Labs’ own social media accounts, such as LinkedIn, X and Instagram, and helps automate related routine work.

This website

tavric-labs.net is a static site. It sets no cookies, has no accounts or forms, and loads no analytics, advertising or other third-party scripts. We do not collect personal information from visitors through the site itself. Like any web server, the host that serves it may keep routine request logs for security and troubleshooting.

Cully

Cully is an internal tool used only by Tavric Labs. When an authorized Tavric Labs team member connects an account (for example by signing in with LinkedIn, X or Instagram and approving access), Cully can act on that account to publish posts, for example a link to a newly published article. It uses only the permissions that person approves, and it does nothing on a platform beyond them.

What we store

  • An access token for each connected account, kept as an encrypted secret in the automation that publishes the post.
  • The account identifier the platform returns, used as the author of the post.
  • A record of what Cully published: the post text and media we wrote, the destination platform, the post link and the time. We may also keep the engagement statistics the platform reports for those posts, such as views and likes, for our own reporting.

We do not read, collect or store the profile data, connections, messages or activity of anyone, including the person who connected the account, beyond what is needed to publish and to record our own posts.

Who uses it

Cully is used only by Tavric Labs, on Tavric Labs’ own accounts and for its own content. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from visitors, followers or other platform members.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose data to third parties. The only recipient of a post’s content is the platform it is published to, which receives it under that platform’s own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and revoking access

Access tokens expire on their own after a limited time and are replaced when the account owner reconnects. Access can be revoked at any time in each platform’s settings, for example in LinkedIn under Settings, Data privacy, Permitted services, or in X and Instagram under connected apps. We delete a stored token on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
