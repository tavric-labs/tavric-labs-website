Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy covers two things Tavric Labs operates: this website, and the Tavric Labs Publisher app, an internal tool that publishes content we have written to our own LinkedIn account.

This website

tavric-labs.net is a static site. It sets no cookies, has no accounts or forms, and loads no analytics, advertising or other third-party scripts. We do not collect personal information from visitors through the site itself. Like any web server, the host that serves it may keep routine request logs for security and troubleshooting.

Tavric Labs Publisher

Tavric Labs Publisher lets an authorized Tavric Labs team member publish content, for example a link to a newly published article, to their own LinkedIn account. When that person authorizes the app with LinkedIn, the app can create posts on their behalf. It does nothing else on LinkedIn.

What we store

  • An OAuth access token for the authorizing account, kept as an encrypted secret in the automation that publishes the post.
  • The identifier LinkedIn returns for that account, used as the author of the post.

We do not read, collect or store the profile data, connections, messages or activity of anyone, including the person who authorized the app, beyond what is needed to create a post.

Who uses it

The app is used only by Tavric Labs to publish its own content. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from visitors or from other LinkedIn members.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose data to third parties. The only recipient of a post’s content is LinkedIn, which receives it to publish the post under LinkedIn’s own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and revoking access

The access token expires on its own after a limited time and is replaced when the account owner re-authorizes. Access can be revoked at any time in LinkedIn under Settings, Data privacy, Permitted services. We delete the stored token on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
